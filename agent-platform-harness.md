## The challenge

Enterprise AI delivery involved repeated hand-offs, undocumented assumptions, and specialist support across disconnected systems.

## The response

I architected an agentic platform and harness that encodes software-delivery standards, reusable skills, governance, and self-service deployment. The design helps teams move from intent to governed delivery without exposing organisation-specific implementation details.

## Selected outcomes

- Reduced a DevOps-supported deployment workflow from up to a week to minutes.
- Enabled applications to move from localhost to enterprise SSO-enabled deployment.
- Embedded access controls, audit logging, vulnerability management, and data-protection safeguards.
- Made risks and architecture trade-offs visible for critical decisions.
