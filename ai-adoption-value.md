## The challenge

Enterprise AI investment was growing, but product teams and leaders lacked a consistent way to distinguish access, meaningful adoption, and realised value.

## The response

I designed an enterprise-wide framework for measuring AI reach, adoption, and business value across a large portfolio of AI and data platforms. The framework connects platform usage and adoption signals to changes in ways of working, productivity, and value, giving leadership a consistent evidence base for AI investment and prioritisation.

## Selected outcomes

- **20+ platforms**.
- **120+ capabilities, tools, and services**.
- Quantifies the breadth and depth of AI adoption across a workforce of **16,000+ people**.
- Provides evidence for product teams and enterprise leadership.

## Why it matters

The framework turns AI adoption from a collection of isolated success stories into a portfolio that can be compared, governed, and improved over time.

> Organisation-specific metrics, platform details, and implementation artefacts are omitted for confidentiality.
