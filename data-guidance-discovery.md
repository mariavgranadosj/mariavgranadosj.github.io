## The challenge

Finding trusted data, platform products, and organisational guidance could take weeks. Existing tools were costly and did not reflect how teams searched or worked.

## The response

As part of a small delivery team, I built semantic discovery across fragmented enterprise knowledge and data. The product joined trusted sources with useful context and gave the team a measurable view of adoption and time to value.

## Selected outcomes

- Avoided more than **$1M** in vendor spend.
- Delivered over **$70K** in measured value in the first month.
- Reduced discovery from weeks to seconds and one workflow from two hours to three seconds.
- Nominated by Snowflake for a global Data Driver award.
